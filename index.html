Questions before you book

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 12 pm and check-out is until 11 am. Need a different time? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to pay now?

No. Your booking is held without payment. You pay at the hotel when you arrive.

How do I cancel or change my booking?

Send your booking reference on WhatsApp at least 48 hours before check-in.

Can I bring children or an extra bed?

Yes. Choose a room that fits your party and mention an extra bed in special requests.
